# Mission Library

Every mission is **one constraint**, shot in one outing. Constraints force growth — they remove the easy options and make you solve the hard part. Each mission names the **dimension** it trains.

> The `next-assignment-coach` reads J's [[Skill Profile]] and picks the next mission: it works the **Fundamentals Track** until those are solid, then targets the **lowest-scoring dimension** (the `growth_edge`), and periodically throws a **genre-stretch** to break ruts. Do the fundamentals in order first — everything else stands on them.

---

## FUNDAMENTALS TRACK (do these first, in order)

These are non-negotiable basics. Until they're ticked in the [[Skill Profile]], the coach keeps returning here.

1. **Exposure-triangle control** — "f/2 all day." Lock aperture at f/2; control exposure only with shutter + ISO. → *Technical* — see [[Exposure Triangle]]
2. **Focus discipline** — "tack-sharp eyes." Single-point AF on the subject's nearest eye every frame; zero soft keepers allowed. → *Technical*
3. **Level horizons** — "spirit level on." No tilted horizons or verticals all outing (use the in-camera level). → *Technical*
4. **One-subject rule** — "one subject only." Every frame has exactly one clear subject; if you can't name it, don't shoot it. → *Subject/Story*
5. **Fill the frame** — "no empty corners (yet)." Get close; the subject must own the frame — feet-not-zoom. → *Composition*
6. **The light test** — "name the light." State direction + quality before every shutter; reject flat front light; find one light beam. → *Light* — see [[Seeing Light]]
7. **Read the histogram** — "read before you click." Check the histogram every frame; never clip the highlight end (blinkies on). → *Technical* — see [[Reading the Histogram]]
8. **Film-sim familiarity** — "know your look." Shoot one outing entirely on [[Classic Negative C7]] and study how it renders skin, sky, shadow, and neon. → *Colour/Tone*

## PER-DIMENSION MISSIONS

### Composition
- "Layers" — every keeper has a clear foreground / midground / background. (→ Composition; see [[Saul Leiter]])
- "Geometry only" — one figure + one strong line/triangle/shadow-shape, deliberate negative space. (→ Composition; see [[Fan Ho]] & [[Composition Basics]])
- "Frame within a frame" — shoot through doorways, arches, railings, gaps.
- "Centre it" — every shot dead-centre symmetrical; make stillness work.
- "Edges patrol" — zero tangents; clean separation at all four edges.

### Light
- "Beam hunter" — only frames where hard light cuts through shadow; figure walks into the wedge. (→ Light; see [[Seeing Light]])
- "Backlight" — every subject lit from behind; meter for the highlight.
- "Golden 40" — shoot only the 40-minute golden window down HDB corridors / five-foot-ways.
- "Window light" — soft side light indoors, no flash, IBIS-assisted.

### Colour/Tone
- "Two colours" — each frame built on a single colour relationship (complementary or analogous).
- "Monochrome eye" — shoot a B&W recipe; learn to see tone without colour.
- "Neon after rain" — wet reflective night colour; grade to [[Classic Negative C7]].
- "Muted only" — find scenes that are *already* low-saturation; no loud colour allowed.

### Subject/Story
- "Decisive moment" — wait for the gesture; the photo must imply a before/after.
- "Hands" — tell the story through hands only.
- "The stranger" — one approached, consented portrait of a stranger with a sense of who they are.
- "Sequence" — five frames that read as a tiny story in order.

### Technical
- "Slow shutter, sharp subject" — drag to 1/15–1/4s, IBIS on, keep the subject sharp / blur the motion.
- "Fill flash, dark background" — leaf-shutter sync at f/2 in daylight; lift subject, crush background. (see [[Fujifilm X100VI]])
- "High ISO night" — ISO 6400, embrace grain, nail focus in the dark.
- "Phone Pro discipline" — entire outing on [[OPPO Find N5]] Pro mode + RAW, manual WB, expose for highlights.

### Post-processing
- "SOOC only" — no editing at all; force it right in-camera. (→ Post, by negative space)
- "Match the Fuji" — grade Find N5 RAWs to sit beside X100VI JPEGs using the [[Singapore Recipe Set]] preset.
- "One look, ten frames" — apply one consistent grade across a whole set for series cohesion.
- "Dodge & burn" — finish three frames with deliberate local light shaping only.

### Genre-craft
- "Street etiquette" — a full candid street set, shot fast and respectfully.
- "Architecture lines" — verticals corrected, geometry-led building studies.
- "Environmental portrait" — subject + the place that explains them.
- "Still life of light" — make an everyday object compelling through light alone.

## GENRE-STRETCH — shoot a genre you avoid

The coach injects one of these to break comfort-zone ruts. Pick whatever is *least* like your usual moody street work.

- **Bright & airy** — high-key, light, joyful; the opposite of crushed shadows.
- **Macro / close-up** — fill the frame with the very small.
- **Sports / action** — fast shutter, prediction, peak-action timing.
- **Food** — controlled light, styling, appetite appeal.
- **Landscape / wide vista** — patience, foreground interest, big skies (East Coast, Southern Ridges, reservoirs).
- **Flash portraiture** — deliberate, lit, posed; off-camera or fill.
- **Documentary essay** — a 10-frame story on one Singapore subject (hawker, wet market, kampong-spirit estate).
- **Abstract** — no recognisable subject; pure shape, colour, and texture.
