# Recipe Creators to Study

Who to follow to deepen your Fujifilm recipe craft — ranked for **your** style (moody, graphic,
light-and-shadow street; Classic Negative; Singapore). Pairs with the recipe library
([[_Recipe Index]]) and your baseline [[Classic Negative C7]].

> **Honest framing first:** recipes are *not* your bottleneck — your Colour/Tone is already Lv4 and the
> Classic-Neg instinct is real. Your logged growth edge is **Composition / Subject-Story** (see
> [[Skill Profile]]). Enjoy the recipe rabbit hole, but the bigger jumps come from framing and timing.

## The shortlist

### 1. Ritchie Roesch — *Fuji X Weekly* ⭐ (you already use his recipes)
- **What:** the definitive Fujifilm recipe resource — 350+ recipes, blog, and app. Your
  [[Kodachrome 64]] and [[Kodak Portra 400 v2]] notes are his.
- **Learn:** breadth of looks; how film stocks map to Fuji settings.
- **Do:** get the **Fuji X Weekly app**, filter by X-Trans 5 / X100VI and by Classic Negative, then
  shoot ONE recipe for a full week.
- **Link:** https://fujixweekly.com

### 2. Pal2tech (Chris Lee) — *best for the WHY*
- **What:** YouTube tutorials that explain what each setting actually does (DR, Clarity, Color Chrome,
  tone curve) instead of just handing values.
- **Learn:** the *lever* behind each number — this is what turns "copying recipes" into "tuning your own".
- **Do:** watch his DR / highlight videos, then go fix the blown-sun problem at capture (DR400 + D-Range
  Priority) rather than in post. See [[Reading the Histogram]] · [[Seeing Light]].

### 3. Omar Gonzalez & Reggie Ballesteros — *field application, street*
- **What:** both shoot Fuji in real street/travel conditions and show recipes in use, not just on a chart.
- **Learn:** how a recipe behaves in messy real light — closest to how you shoot Singapore streets.

### 4. Anders Lindborg — *B&W / Acros* (your [[Kodak Tri-X 400]] recipe is his)
- **What:** high-contrast monochrome Acros recipes, often via Fuji X Weekly.
- **Learn:** the B&W direction the coach keeps suggesting for your backlit / contre-jour frames —
  silhouettes + grids are made for mono.

### 5. Kevin Mullins — *B&W documentary master*
- **What:** Fuji X-Photographer, Acros-based documentary/wedding work.
- **Learn:** committing fully to monochrome storytelling, if mono becomes a serious lane.

## How to actually improve (not just collect)
- **One recipe, one week** — master a few rather than hoarding many.
- **Build variations of [[Classic Negative C7]]** (your "C7 Contre-Jour", "Atrium Lattice" deltas)
  instead of chasing new looks.
- **Learn the lever, not the number** — once you know *why* a setting works, you fix problems at
  capture, not in a recipe.
- **Watch SOOC** (Straight Out Of Camera) — the live Fuji X Weekly recipe show — for recipe-in-practice.
