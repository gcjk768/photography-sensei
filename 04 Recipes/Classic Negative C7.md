# Classic Negative C7 (J's Baseline)

> [!note] J's own recipe
> This is **J's personal baseline recipe**, not a Fuji X Weekly recipe. It lives on custom slot **C7** of the X100VI and is the default look J reaches for first. Tune from here rather than starting from scratch.

## Settings (X100VI — copy exactly)

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Film Simulation | Classic Negative |

| Color Chrome Effect | Strong |

## Personality

Soft, dreamy, and nostalgic. The Highlight −2 / Clarity −4 combination rolls off harsh edges and gives skies and skin a gentle, faded-film glow. It sings in directional warm light — golden hour, backlit subjects, and the dramatic raking light before or after a storm — where the muted Classic Negative palette turns ordinary scenes cinematic. The look intentionally trades crispness and contrast for mood.

## Watch-points

- **DR100 + JPEG-only cannot rescue blown highlights.** Once the highlight is clipped in-camera there is no RAW to recover. In high-contrast scenes meter for the highlights (slight negative exposure compensation) or step up to DR200/DR400.
- **Color +2 can push greens lurid** — foliage and grass can go neon under strong sun. Drop Color toward 0 for lush parks (see Botanic Green in the Singapore set).
- **Clarity −4 turns to mud in haze.** On flat, hazy days the negative clarity removes the little micro-contrast there is, leaving the frame soft and lifeless. Switch to positive clarity in haze (see Haze Cutter).
- **AUTO-UPDATE ON SILENTLY OVERWRITES C7.** With *Auto Update Custom Setting* enabled, any in-the-moment dial tweak is written back into the slot — so your "baseline" quietly drifts. **For a fixed, trustworthy reference recipe, DISABLE Auto Update Custom Setting** so C7 always means exactly the values above.
